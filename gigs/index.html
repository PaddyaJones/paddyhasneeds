      Paddy's Gig Log

      Every show I could find a ticket for, from Anathallo at the Borderline in 2008 to whatever's on next week in Brooklyn. Festivals and club nights are logged as events. A band only counts as seen at a festival if I called them out at the time, since nobody remembers exactly who they saw at Gov Ball.

      Tap any name for that artist's page: every time I've seen them, photos from Instagram, and where they turn up in my albums of the year.
